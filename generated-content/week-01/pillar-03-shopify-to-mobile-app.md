## How to Convert Shopify to Mobile App (Step-by-Step)

Let's build your Shopify app using WebsiteToApp:

### Step 1: Prepare Your Shopify Store

Optimize your store before creating the app:

#### Product Images
- **Requirement:** Square product images (1:1 ratio)
- **Size:** At least 1000x1000px
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Tip:** Use consistent backgrounds (white works best)

#### Product Descriptions
- Write for mobile (shorter paragraphs)
- Include key details first
- Use bullet points
- Mobile users skim!

#### Collections
- Organize products into clear categories
- Create featured collections (new arrivals, bestsellers)
- Use descriptive collection names

#### Checkout Settings
- Go to **Settings → Checkout**
- Enable "Use same address for billing"
- Add trust badges
- Offer guest checkout

**Time:** 1-2 hours (if optimizing)

### Step 5: Set Up Push Notifications for Sales

This is where apps crush mobile websites:

#### Notification Types:

**1. Flash Sales & Promotions**
- **When:** Launching a sale
- **Example:** "⚡ Flash Sale! 40% Off Everything - 24 Hours Only"
- **Expected:** 23% open rate, 8% purchase rate

**2. New Product Launches**
- **When:** Adding new products
- **Example:** "🆕 New Arrivals Just Dropped! Shop Now"
- **Expected:** 31% open rate, 12% conversion

**3. Abandoned Cart Recovery**
- **When:** User leaves items in cart
- **Timing:** 1 hour, then 24 hours later
- **Example:** "🛒 You left items in your cart! Complete your purchase now"
- **Expected:** 45% open rate, 15% recovery rate

**4. Back in Stock**
- **When:** Popular item restocked
- **Example:** "✨ [Product Name] is Back in Stock!"
- **Expected:** 52% open rate, 28% purchase rate

**5. Price Drops**
- **When:** Product goes on sale
- **Example:** "💰 Price Drop Alert! [Product] now $X (was $Y)"
- **Expected:** 38% open rate, 19% conversion

**6. Personalized Recommendations**
- **When:** Based on browsing history
- **Example:** "We think you'll love these..."
- **Expected:** 27% open rate, 9% conversion

**7. Order Updates**
- **When:** Order shipped, delivered
- **Example:** "📦 Your order has shipped! Track here"
- **Expected:** 76% open rate (informational)

#### Notification Best Practices:
- ✅ Send 1-3 per week max (don't spam!)
- ✅ Test send times (2 PM and 7 PM work best)
- ✅ Personalize when possible
- ✅ Always include discount/urgency
- ✅ Track which notifications convert best

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Does a Shopify app replace my website?

**A:** No! Your Shopify website and app work together. The app is an additional sales channel, like adding Instagram Shopping or Amazon. Most sales still come from your website—the app captures mobile users who prefer apps.

### Q: Can I use my existing Shopify checkout?

**A:** Yes! The app uses Shopify's checkout system, so all your payment gateways, shipping settings, and tax calculations work exactly the same.

### Q: What happens to orders placed in the app?

**A:** They appear in your Shopify admin just like website orders. You fulfill them the same way—no separate process needed.

### Q: Will my inventory sync in real-time?

**A:** Yes! When someone buys a product in the app, your Shopify inventory decreases instantly. Same when you update stock in Shopify—the app reflects it immediately.

### Q: Can I run sales and promotions?

**A:** Absolutely! Create discount codes in Shopify, then promote them via push notifications. Or set sale prices in Shopify and they'll show in the app automatically.

### Q: Do Shopify apps work with my theme?

**A:** The app creates its own native shopping interface, so your theme doesn't directly affect the app. However, your product images, descriptions, and branding carry over.

### Q: Can international customers use my app?

**A:** Yes! If your Shopify store ships internationally, the app works globally. You can send localized push notifications and support multiple currencies.

### Q: How do app users differ from website users?

**A:** App users spend 30% more, buy 3x more frequently, and have 72% higher lifetime value. They're typically your most engaged customers.

### Q: Can I track app sales separately?

**A:** Yes! Orders are tagged in Shopify admin so you can filter by channel (app vs website). You can also integrate Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel for detailed tracking.

### Q: What if I change Shopify plans?

**A:** Your app continues to work seamlessly. Whether you're on Basic, Shopify, or Advanced, the app functions the same.
