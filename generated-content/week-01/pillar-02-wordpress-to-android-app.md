## WordPress to App: Common Questions

### Q: Will my WordPress site still work after making an app?

**A:** Yes! Your WordPress site remains completely unchanged. The app simply displays your site's content in a native app wrapper. You continue managing everything from WordPress as usual.

### Q: Do I need to publish posts twice (WordPress + App)?

**A:** No! Post once in WordPress, and it automatically appears in the app within seconds. No duplicate work needed.

### Q: What happens when I update a WordPress post?

**A:** The app reflects changes instantly (or within a few minutes depending on caching). No need to republish the app to Google Play for content updates.

### Q: Can I use Gutenberg, Elementor, or Divi?

**A:** Yes! Any WordPress page builder works. The app displays the final rendered HTML, so it doesn't matter how you create content.

### Q: Will my theme affect the app?

**A:** Partially. The app uses your theme's styling (colors, fonts) but wraps it in native app navigation. Mobile-responsive themes work best.

### Q: Can users comment on posts in the app?

**A:** Yes! WordPress comments work normally. Users can read and post comments just like on the website.

### Q: Does the app work with WordPress multisite?

**A:** Yes! Each site in your multisite network can have its own app, or you can create one app for the main site.

### Q: Can I password-protect content?

**A:** Yes, WordPress password-protected posts work. Users will see a login prompt when accessing protected content.

### Q: Will my analytics track app users?

**A:** Yes! If you use Google Analytics or Jetpack Stats, app visitors are tracked separately. You can also integrate Firebase Analytics for app-specific metrics.

### Q: What about WordPress.com vs WordPress.org?

**A:** Both work perfectly! Whether you use WordPress.com (hosted) or self-hosted WordPress.org, the app conversion process is identical.
