## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What's the cheapest way to convert website to app?

**A:** The absolute cheapest is DIY with Android Studio (free), but requires coding skills and 20+ hours. For non-developers, WebsiteToApp's free plan ($0) is the best option. You can create a fully functional Android app without paying anything.

**A:** Yes, if you use reputable platforms like WebsiteToApp. Free plans are safe but may include platform branding and limited features. Always read reviews and check security certifications before uploading your website.

### Q: Do I need to pay separately for Android and iOS apps?

**A:** It depends on the platform. WebsiteToApp charges one price for both Android and Windows support. iOS is coming soon. However, you'll pay Apple's $99/year developer fee regardless of which platform you use.

### Q: Are there any hidden fees I should know about?

**A:** The main "hidden" costs are app store fees ($25 for Google Play, $99/year for Apple). Some platforms also charge extra for premium features like white labeling, unlimited apps, or priority support. WebsiteToApp includes most features in standard plans.

**A:** With no-code builders, maintenance is included in your monthly fee. With custom development, budget 15-20% of initial development cost annually ($1,500-10,000/year for a $10,000 app).

### Q: Can I switch platforms later without starting over?

**A:** Yes! Since your app pulls content from your website, you can switch app platforms anytime. Your website stays the same, and you just rebuild the app wrapper with a new platform.

### Q: What if I need features not included in my plan?

**A:** You can upgrade to a higher tier plan, request custom features (may cost extra), or switch to a different platform. With WebsiteToApp, most features are included even in lower-tier plans.

### Q: Is it cheaper to build once or pay monthly?

**A:** Monthly no-code plans are cheaper long-term. A $10,000 custom app costs the same as 41 years of WebsiteToApp Pro ($9.99/mo). Plus, no-code includes updates and hosting, which cost extra with custom development.

### Q: Do app builders offer refunds?

**A:** Most offer free trials or money-back guarantees. WebsiteToApp has a free plan so you can test before paying. Check each platform's refund policy before subscribing.

### Q: Can I make money with a free app?

**A:** Yes! Free apps can monetize through in-app ads (AdMob), affiliate links, or driving traffic to your website where you sell products/services. Paid plans offer more monetization options like in-app purchases.
