# WebToApp Platform Guide

WebToApp is a developer service that converts standard web applications or website URLs into native Android (.apk, .aab) and desktop applications.

## Product Features
* **Branding**: Configure custom app name, package name, application launcher icons, and splash screen animations.
* **Branding Options**: Custom loading indicators and custom primary colors.
* **Integrations**: Supports offline caching/local mode, file download manager, file upload chooser, camera/location access, deep linking, passcode lock, push notifications, and AdMob ads integrations.
* **Store Listing**: Basic builds are delivered directly. Play Store Listing add-on includes hand-held assistance uploading AAB bundles to Google Play Console.

## Pricing Plans & Limits
* **Free Plan**: 2 free builds (Android or Desktop), watermarked splash screen, no payment required.
* **Premium Plan — Android App ($25 one-time)**: 5 rebuilds/month per URL, full features, custom branding, push notifications, AdMob, Firebase, custom keystore support. No subscription.
* **Premium Plan — Desktop App ($25 one-time)**: Same as Android but outputs a Windows desktop app (.exe installer). 5 rebuilds/month.
* **Play Store Listing Add-on ($15 one-time)**: We publish your app to Google Play Store on your behalf.
* **Android App + Play Store Bundle ($40 one-time)**: Premium Android App + Play Store Listing at a discounted bundle price.

## Key Policy Notes
* All paid plans are **one-time payments** — no subscriptions, no renewals.
* Free plan users get 2 builds total; paid plan users get 5 rebuilds per 30 rolling days.
* Custom keystore upload is available on paid plans via the Advanced Settings step during app creation.
* Refunds are handled case-by-case. Contact support@websitetoapp.app.
