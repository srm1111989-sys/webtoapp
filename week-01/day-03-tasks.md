## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Should I include competitor names in pricing page?**
A: Yes! It helps with SEO ("websitetoapp vs median pricing") and provides value.

**Q: How detailed should the outline be?**
A: Very! Include H2s, H3s, key points per section, word count targets.
