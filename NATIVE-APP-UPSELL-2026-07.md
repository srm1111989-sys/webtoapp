# Native App Development Upsell (draft 2026-07-13)

Target: free/paid WebToApp users whose websites are real businesses on custom domains —
candidates for a paid custom native app project (beyond the WebView wrapper).

**Do NOT send to:** test accounts, piracy/adult/impersonation apps (same exclusion list
as SHARE-FOR-UPGRADE-CAMPAIGN.md), or users already in an active support dispute.

**Timing note:** the share-for-upgrade email went out 2026-07-13. Space this one at
least 5–7 days later so users don't get two asks in one week. Prioritize anyone who
responded to campaign #1 (proven engaged).

---

## Email draft

**Subject options:**
- Your app {app_name} — want to take it fully native?
- We build custom native apps too — interested?

**Body:**

Hi {first_name},

I'm Swapnil, founder of WebToApp — you built **{app_name}** with us.

A quick question rather than a pitch: some of our users outgrow the website-in-an-app
approach and want a **fully native app** — real native screens, faster performance,
offline features, deeper device integration (camera flows, background sync, payments),
and a Play Store presence that feels like a product, not a wrapper.

We've started offering **custom native app development** for exactly that. Since
{app_name} looks like a real business, I wanted to ask:

- Is a native app something you've considered?
- What would it need to do that your current app can't?

If it's interesting, reply and I'll send an honest estimate (timeline + fixed price —
no discovery-call runaround). If not, no worries — your current app keeps working
as-is, and we're here either way.

Best,
Swapnil
WebToApp · https://websitetoapp.app
